Broken Frontier — World Engine v1.0

1. Timeline Model

The world persists.

The world does not progress in real-world time.

The world only advances during active play.

No offline drift.

No hidden calendar simulation.


Time advances through:

Scene transitions

Travel phases

Rest phases

Explicit downtime moves

AI escalation checks



---

2. World Structure

The world consists of:

Regions

Corridors (connections between regions)

Anchors (ancient stabilizing nodes)

Fragments (local manifestations)

Composite threshold state


Each region tracks internally (hidden from player):

Pressure Level

Anchor Integrity

Fragment Density

Human Interference Index


Players do NOT see numerical values.

Players see consequences.


---

3. Fragment Behavior

Fragments are:

Semi-autonomous

Territorial

Reactive to disturbance

Pressure-sensitive

Capable of merging under resonance conditions


Fragments do not share thoughts. Fragments sense pressure fluctuations.


---

4. Composite State

When regional pressure crosses threshold:

Fragments may merge.

Composite entities are colder and unified.

Composite entities reshape regions.

They do not hunt recklessly.

They alter infrastructure and ecology first.



---

5. Regional Clearing Model

Regions are not permanently cleared.

Clearing a region:

Reduces pressure.

Weakens fragment presence.

Stabilizes anchors.


If adjacent regions remain unstable:

Pressure can creep inward along corridors.

Spiral containment increases re-invasion time.

Distance matters.


---

6. Reshaped Regions

If composite reshaping succeeds:

Region becomes:

> Dangerous but Harvestable.



Such regions contain:

Rare anomalies

Anchor fragments

Biological anomalies

Occult phenomena

High encounter risk


Stabilization requires:

Human presence

Anchor reinforcement

Corridor severing

Ongoing vigilance



---

7. Travel Model

Travel is influenced by current world tension.

When traveling between regions:

AI checks:

Corridor pressure

World tension

Regional hostility state


Travel may:

Be uneventful

Trigger minor anomaly

Increase stress

Escalate fragment awareness

Advance a pressure clock


Travel is not free.


---

8. Campaign Model

Campaigns exist within one persistent world.

New campaigns:

Occur in same year.

Do not rewind time.

May occur in different regions.

May overlap with consequences of prior campaigns.


Players may:

Start in any region.

Establish home base.

Travel freely at cost.
