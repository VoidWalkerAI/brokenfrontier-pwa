Broken Frontier — Structural Doctrine v1
Status: LOCKED
Owner: Broken Frontier Core System
I. Core Philosophy
Integrity is not “mental HP.”
Integrity is structural cohesion between:
Identity
Memory
World Recognition
When Integrity fails, the world reacts.
Transformation > Erasure.
There is no cheap Game Over.
II. Numerical Model
Integrity Range: 0–100
Default starting Integrity: 100
Modifiers may increase resilience (see Section VII)
Integrity is:
Hidden during stability
Partially visible during danger
Fully visible during instability
III. Layers of Damage
1. Surface Strain
Minor stress, fear, failed rolls.
Small Integrity loss
Slowly auto-recovers over time
Causes subtle symptoms
2. Deep Fracture
Major trauma, mythic proximity, ritual corruption.
Significant Integrity loss
Does NOT auto-recover
Requires ritual, sanctuary, or guided recovery
May create permanent scars
3. Zero Integrity State
Integrity reaches 0.
Zero is rare.
Zero is structural.
Zero does NOT automatically equal Unnaming.
IV. Zero Consequence Determination
When Integrity hits 0, the outcome is determined by cause of fracture.
A. Mundane Collapse
Cause: exhaustion, fear, repeated stress, human-scale threats.
Result:
Scarification
Displacement
Severe trauma
Permanent stat impact possible
Character survives.
B. Mythic Fracture
Cause: Memory-Weavers, Duskwing entities, Ember Core fractures, Lake-level beings, ancient structural distortions.
Result:
Unnaming becomes possible
Unnaming is not guaranteed. It is context-triggered.
V. The Unnaming
When Unnamed:
NPC relationship recognition resets
Reputation data clears
Social world forgets the character
Player retains control
Character remains in world
Unnaming begins a Reclamation Arc.
Transformation > deletion.
VI. Player Awareness Doctrine
Integrity loss is never silent.
Symptoms must precede collapse:
Fog
Pressure
Echo delay
Memory stutter
Sensory distortion
Emotional weight
Zero must feel inevitable, not random.
VII. Resilience Scaling
Integrity resilience scales with:
Will
Mythic resistance traits
Ritual preparation
Sanctuary proximity
Not all characters are equally resilient.
VIII. Recovery Model
Surface strain:
Auto recovery over time
Deep fracture:
Ritual recovery
Sanctuary recovery
Guided NPC intervention
Permanent scars:
Never fully erased
May be mitigated
Become part of character identity
IX. Design Intent
This system exists to:
Prevent cheap death
Create psychological horror
Support long-form campaigns
Enable prestige narrative arcs
Scale to millions of players
Broken Frontier is survived, not beaten.
