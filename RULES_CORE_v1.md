# Broken Frontier — RULES CORE v1 (Locked)

## Core Resolution
- Default resolution die: d20
- The GM calls for rolls only when outcome is uncertain + meaningful.
- Player can roll physical dice and enter the natural result.

## Target Numbers (TN)
- 8  Easy (routine under stress)
- 12 Standard (most tense actions)
- 15 Hard (combat pressure, darkness, confusion)
- 18 Extreme (heroic, brutal, razor-thin)

## Total
total = natural_d20 + STAT + MOD - PENALTY

### STAT
One of: Grit, Instinct, Will, Presence, Discipline

### PENALTY (pressure tax)
penalty = Wounds + floor(Stress / 3)

## Degrees (Margin = total - TN)
- margin >= +5 : STRONG success (win ground, gain advantage)
- margin 0..+4 : success (you do it, but time/position may shift)
- margin -1..-4 : fail (complication, cost, or lost position)
- margin <= -5 : severe fail (danger escalates, clock jumps, exposed)

## Criticals (Natural d20)
- Nat 20: critical — add a benefit (extra effect, reduced cost, or immediate advantage)
- Nat 1: catastrophic — GM must apply a hard consequence (Exposed, clock jump, or Wound/Stress)

## Harm Model
- HP represents immediate fight capacity.
- Wounds represent lasting injury and fragility.
- Stress represents fear, pressure, mental fatigue.

### Taking Damage
- Damage reduces HP first.
- If damage would drop HP below 0:
  - HP becomes 0
  - gain +1 Wound
  - any leftover damage becomes +Stress (or narrative harm)

### 0 HP State
- At 0 HP you are compromised (staggered, pinned, bleeding, collapsing).
- Another meaningful hit at 0 HP risks:
  - +1 Wound (again)
  - incapacitation
  - or death if fiction demands and the player keeps choosing danger

## Recovery (v1 baseline)
- HP can recover in-scene only by taking refuge / cover / time, limited.
- Wounds do not disappear mid-fight.
- Stress can be reduced by rest, safety, ritual, or successful grounding actions.

## Tone & Fairness
- The system never cheats.
- NPCs can lie. The system does not falsify rolls, stats, or hidden edits.
- Horror comes from pressure + consequence + intelligence, not random gotchas.
